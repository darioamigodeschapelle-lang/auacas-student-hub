# Privacy Notice — Alpha

The AUACAS Student Hub alpha is a static, read-only informational website.

This version does not use analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, student accounts, forms, uploads, or databases. It does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, questions, files, or browsing histories.

GitHub Pages, as the hosting provider, may process limited technical information for security, reliability, and delivery under GitHub's own policies.

This is a student-led alpha and is not an official AUACAS publication.
