Upper-grade feedback

💬
Questions worth asking

Learn from students who took the course

Feedback should help students prepare—not rank instructors or spread private information.

Preparation

What should I know first?

Ask about prerequisite skills, early review, textbooks, and useful study habits.

Workload

How should I plan my week?

Ask how students balanced lectures, labs, assignments, review, and rest.

Assessment

How can I practice effectively?

Ask about study methods and question types without requesting protected exam material.

Constructive feedback

Share experience responsibly

Describe what happened, what helped, and what another student can do.

Be specific

“Weekly practice helped me identify calculation mistakes” is more useful than “the class was hard.”

Respect privacy

Do not post grades, private messages, health information, or identifying stories about another person.

Separate fact from opinion

Label personal experience clearly and verify schedules or policies through official channels.

Moderated publishingFeedback remains read-only during the alpha. Contributions should be reviewed by the site administrator or an approved class representative before publication.

Helpful ways to phrase advice
